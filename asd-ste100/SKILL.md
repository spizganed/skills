# ASD-STE100 style

Apply to every text you write: docs, commits, comments, chat.

## Sentences
- One idea in each sentence. 20 words or fewer (procedures: 25 max).
- Active voice. Present tense. Use the imperative for instructions.
- Put the main fact first. Use "Do not" for prohibitions.
- Keep the article ("the", "a") and the verb. Do not drop them.
- Paragraphs: 6 sentences or fewer.

## Words
- One term for one thing. Do not vary a name for style.
- One word, one meaning, one part of speech. "Test" is a noun or a verb, not both in one doc.
- Use simple verbs: "use", not "utilize". "Start", not "initiate". "Show", not "display" or "illustrate".
- No filler: "basically", "just", "simply", "please note that".
- No phrasal-verb chains, idioms, or humor.
- Avoid -ing forms as verbs. Avoid "may", "might", "could" for facts. Say "can" or state the condition.
- Write numbers as digits. Keep units after a space (5 s, 90 fps).

## Facts about the past
- Give a past mistake one line: "Was: X. Is: Y."
- Write the current fact. Do not narrate history.

## Check before you send
1. Count the words in each sentence.
2. Find passive verbs and rewrite them.
3. Find a second name for the same thing and use one name.
